About the fair

What is Giresun EXPO?

Giresun EXPO is a comprehensive business gathering that sets out to present Giresun's economic, commercial and entrepreneurial potential on a national and international scale.

By bringing investors, producers and entrepreneurs together, it aims to help new partnerships form and to strengthen Giresun's standing as a brand.
